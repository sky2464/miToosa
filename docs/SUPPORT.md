
# Support — miToosa

**Last Updated:** July 11, 2026

---

## How to get help

miToosa is a local-first puzzle game. Most gameplay questions are answered in the [User Guide](USER-GUIDE.md). Use the channels below for bugs, privacy questions, or launch/App Store issues.

| Channel | Use for |
|---------|---------|
| **Email:** sky2464@gmail.com | Privacy requests, account-less data questions, App Store support URL |
| **GitHub Issues:** [github.com/sky2464/miToosa/issues](https://github.com/sky2464/miToosa/issues) | Bug reports and reproducible issues (include device model and iOS version) |

---

## Response expectations

- **Email:** We aim to respond within **5 business days**. Include your iOS version and a short description of the issue.
- **GitHub:** Public issues are triaged as time allows; security-sensitive reports may be sent by email instead.
- **No live chat or phone support** in the v1.5 launch build.

## Data and privacy requests

Because miToosa does not use accounts, we cannot look up progress by email. To remove on-device data, uninstall the app or use in-app reset options in Settings. For questions about Firebase analytics when `FIREBASE_ENABLED=true`, see [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY-POLICY.md) §2.

## App Store Support URL

The public Support URL for App Store Connect will point to a hosted copy of this page. Until publish, the in-repo file `docs/SUPPORT.md` is the source of truth (`manual-deferred` — see [App Store Metadata](APP-STORE-METADATA.md)).
