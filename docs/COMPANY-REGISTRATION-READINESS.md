# Company Registration Readiness

This is the owner checklist for preparing company formation and App Store seller readiness. It is not legal advice.

## Formation decisions

- [ ] Legal entity name.
- [ ] DBA / trade-name decision for `miToosa`.
- [ ] Formation state.
- [ ] Registered agent.
- [ ] Principal business address.
- [ ] Business mailing address, if different.
- [ ] Organizer/member/manager information.
- [ ] Ownership percentages and control rights.

## Public contact surfaces

- [ ] Company domain.
- [ ] Support email.
- [ ] Legal/privacy email.
- [ ] Privacy Policy URL.
- [ ] Support URL.
- [ ] Optional marketing/product URL.
- [ ] Phone/address posture for App Store/customer contact requirements.

## Post-formation admin

- [ ] EIN.
- [ ] Business bank account.
- [ ] Bookkeeping system.
- [ ] Password manager vault for company assets.
- [ ] Tax/accounting owner.
- [ ] Calendar reminders for annual reports, registered-agent renewal, domain renewal, and Apple Developer Program renewal.

## IP and asset ownership

- [ ] Source-code ownership record.
- [ ] App name ownership record.
- [ ] Domain ownership record.
- [ ] Design/source asset ownership record.
- [ ] Font and icon license inventory.
- [ ] App Store screenshots and marketing asset ownership record.
- [ ] Apple Distribution certificate backup policy.
- [ ] Firebase/Google account ownership and recovery policy.

## App Store seller readiness

- [ ] Decide final seller name after company registration.
- [ ] Confirm Apple account holder/admin uses the company identity.
- [ ] Keep legal name, public support contact, and privacy policy contact consistent.
- [ ] Keep App Store metadata aligned with company ownership and public URLs.
