
# User Guide — miToosa

**Version:** 1.2.0

---

## What is miToosa?

miToosa is an IQ puzzle game with 23 unique tracks, each testing a different cognitive skill. Solve puzzles, earn XP, build streaks, and track your progress.

---

## Getting Started

When you first open miToosa, a 3-page onboarding walkthrough introduces:

1. **Welcome** — What miToosa is about
2. **How to Play** — Tap the correct answer before time runs out
3. **Tracks** — Browse puzzle categories and pick your challenge

After onboarding, you'll land on the **Tracks** tab.

---

## Main Tabs

miToosa has three tabs at the bottom of the screen:

### Tracks
Browse all 23 puzzle tracks. Each track card shows:
- Track name and icon
- Number of levels
- Your star progress

Tap a track to see its levels and start playing.

### Progress
View your stats:
- **XP** — earned by completing levels
- **Hearts** — 5 max; spend one per attempt; refuel over time or by sharing
- **Diamonds** — bonus currency earned via streaks
- **Streak** — consecutive correct answers; longer streaks earn more rewards
- **Levels Completed** — total across all tracks

### Leaderboard
Coming soon in v1.3. Currently shows a placeholder.

---

## Gameplay

1. A puzzle is presented based on the track's rule type
2. Choose the correct answer from the options shown
3. If stuck, tap **Hint** — one wrong option is greyed out
4. Answer before the timer runs out
5. Earn 1–5 stars based on speed and accuracy

### Training Mode
Levels 0–2 in each track are simplified for new players, with easier puzzles and more generous timing.

## Hearts & Sharing

- You start with **5 hearts**
- Each level attempt costs 1 heart
- Hearts refuel automatically over time
- **Share to refuel**: Tap the share button to share miToosa with a friend and earn hearts back (once per day)

---

## Adaptive Difficulty

miToosa adjusts to your skill level. If you're consistently scoring high, puzzles get harder. If you're struggling, they ease up. This happens automatically based on your recent performance.

---

## Resetting Progress

Go to **Settings** (gear icon) and tap **Reset Progress**. This will:
- Clear all XP, stars, and streak data
- Generate a new player ID
- You cannot undo this action

---

## Privacy

miToosa stores all data locally on your device. No data is sent to any server. See [PRIVACY-POLICY.md](PRIVACY-POLICY.md) for full details.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| App won't open | Force-close and reopen; ensure your OS is up to date |
| Progress lost | Check that you haven't cleared app data in device settings |
| No sound | Check device volume and ensure miToosa audio is not muted |
| Hearts stuck at 0 | Wait for automatic refuel, or share the app to earn hearts |
